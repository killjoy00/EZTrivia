EZ Trivia

Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026

EZ Trivia is a trivia game. This policy explains the information used when you play, view ads, or use Game Center.

Information EZ Trivia uses

  • Scores and preferences. Your personal scores are stored on your device. You can erase them from the Leaderboard screen at any time.
  • Game Center. If you sign in to Apple Game Center, EZ Trivia submits your score to the leaderboard for the category you played, and to the daily leaderboard for daily challenge rounds. Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard data under Apple's privacy policy. EZ Trivia does not receive your Apple ID credentials.
  • Analytics and crash diagnostics. EZ Trivia ships no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no gameplay events anywhere. Apple provides the developer with aggregate App Store analytics and opt-in crash reports; that data comes from Apple, not from the app, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Advertising. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other data needed to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, consistent with the choices you make in the in-app consent prompt.

Tracking and consent

EZ Trivia does not directly sell personal information. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to collect your advertising consent choices where required, and honors that choice on every ad request.

Local flag artwork

National flag images are bundled with the app. Viewing a flag question does not contact a flag-image server.

Retention and deletion

Local scores remain on your device until you clear them or delete the app. Game Center leaderboard data is managed through Apple. Advertising data retention is controlled through Google AdMob's account settings and applicable terms.

Children

EZ Trivia is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can play without Game Center by declining sign-in, clear your local scores inside the app at any time, change your ad consent choice from the Scores tab, limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, and delete the app to remove its local data. Requests concerning data processed by Apple or Google should be directed to those providers.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to killjoy00@yahoo.com or filed at github.com/killjoy00/EZTrivia/issues. Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
