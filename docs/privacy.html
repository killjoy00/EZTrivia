EZ Trivia

Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

EZ Trivia is a trivia game. This policy explains the information used when you play, sync progress, view ads, report a question, or use platform gaming services.

Information EZ Trivia uses

  • Player progress and preferences. Scores, Daily Challenge history, Friend Challenge results, Quick Play history, seen-question state, lifetime points, and achievement progress are stored on your device. On iOS, when iCloud is available, EZ Trivia also uses Apple's private iCloud key-value storage to synchronize gameplay progress across devices signed in to the same iCloud account. On Android, when Google Play Games is connected and Saved Games is available, EZ Trivia stores a private Play Games saved-game snapshot containing gameplay history, question-progress facts, seen-question state, lifetime points, achievement-related progress, and merge bookkeeping needed to preserve independent offline progress from multiple devices. The developer does not operate a separate EZ Trivia account system or player-state server and does not receive your Apple ID or Google Account credentials.
  • Apple Game Center. On iOS, if you sign in to Apple Game Center, EZ Trivia submits category lifetime-point totals, Daily Challenge weighted scores, and configured Game Center achievement progress. Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard/achievement data under Apple's privacy policy. EZ Trivia does not receive your Apple ID credentials.
  • Google Play Games Services. On Android, if your Google Play Games profile is available to EZ Trivia, Play Games Services handles platform authentication. EZ Trivia submits configured achievement progress, category lifetime-point totals, and the current Daily Challenge weighted score to Google Play Games Services. When Saved Games is available, EZ Trivia also reads and writes the private progress snapshot described above. Google processes your Play Games identity, leaderboard/achievement data, and Saved Games data under Google's applicable privacy terms. EZ Trivia does not receive your Google Account password.
  • Question reports. If you choose Report this question, EZ Trivia opens your email app with a draft addressed to support. The draft includes the question ID, prompt, selected and correct answers, explanation, difficulty, and app version so the issue can be investigated. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.
  • Analytics and crash diagnostics. EZ Trivia ships no first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no gameplay events to the developer. Platform operators may provide aggregate store analytics or opt-in diagnostics under their own privacy terms.
  • Purchases. The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple through StoreKit on iOS and by Google through Google Play Billing on Android. EZ Trivia never sees or stores payment details; it records a local entitlement status so paid users do not briefly see ads while the platform store rechecks ownership, and the platform store remains authoritative for restores, refunds, and revocations.
  • Advertising. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other data needed to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, consistent with the privacy choices presented in the app where advertising is enabled.

Tracking and consent

EZ Trivia does not directly sell personal information. Where advertising is enabled, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable advertising privacy choices.

Platform progress sync

On iOS, iCloud player-state synchronization is provided by Apple and tied to the iCloud account configured on the device. On Android, Google Play Games Saved Games provides private player-progress synchronization when the player is connected to Play Games and the platform service is available. Android keeps local gameplay state as the offline-first source of truth and merges cloud snapshots rather than requiring network access to play. If a platform service is unavailable, core trivia gameplay continues with local progress and sync can retry later.

Local flag artwork

National flag images are bundled with the app. Viewing a flag question does not contact a flag-image server.

Retention and deletion

Recent category-round history can be cleared from the Scores screen. Other gameplay facts are retained locally as player progress. iCloud-synchronized data may remain associated with your iCloud account under Apple's controls. Google Play Games Saved Games, leaderboard, and achievement data may remain associated with your Play Games profile under Google's controls. Advertising data retention is controlled through Google AdMob and its applicable terms.

Children

EZ Trivia is not intended to collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can use the app without a separate EZ Trivia account, clear recent category-round history inside the app, manage iCloud and Game Center through Apple settings, manage Play Games profile and sign-in behavior through Google Play Games settings, manage available advertising privacy choices in the app where applicable, and delete the app to remove its local data. Platform-synchronized data may remain with Apple or Google under their account and service controls. Requests concerning data processed by Apple or Google should also be directed to those providers.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to killjoy00@yahoo.com or filed at github.com/killjoy00/EZTrivia/issues. Material changes will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
