Common questions

How does Quick Play work?

Quick Play gives you ten different categories in one round. It starts with three Easy questions, moves through four Medium questions, and finishes with three Hard questions. It prefers questions you have not recently seen.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone on the same app challenge version who plays on a given day answers the same ten questions, in the same order. It resets at your local midnight. Your completed result is saved for that day; if you leave before finishing, you can restart the round from the beginning.

My streak disappeared. What happened?

A streak only breaks once you've missed a full day. If you played yesterday and haven't played today yet, your streak is still intact — you have until the next local midnight to keep it.

How do Friend Challenges work?

Finish a Friend Challenge and share the web link. A friend with EZ Trivia installed can open the exact same questions from that link. The short challenge code is included as a fallback and can be entered manually from Friend Challenge in the app.

Does my progress sync between devices?

When iCloud is available, EZ Trivia synchronizes private gameplay progress such as scores, Daily and Friend Challenge history, Quick Play history, lifetime points, seen questions, and achievement progress across devices signed in to the same iCloud account. The game remains fully playable with local storage when iCloud is unavailable.

Do I need to sign in to play?

No. Category rounds, Quick Play, Daily Challenge, and Friend Challenges work without Game Center. Signing in adds Game Center leaderboards and synchronization for the configured Game Center achievements.

My leaderboard score looks strange.

Category leaderboards rank lifetime points, not a single round's percentage — harder questions are worth more, and the number only ever goes up as you keep playing. The Daily Challenge leaderboard is separate and resets each day.

Can I turn off sound, haptics, or auto-advance?

Yes. Open the Settings tab to control sound effects, haptics, auto-advance, and Daily Challenge streak reminders.

I found a wrong answer or a typo.

After answering a question, tap Report this question beneath its explanation. EZ Trivia opens a prefilled email containing the stable question ID and answer details so you do not have to copy them yourself. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email.
