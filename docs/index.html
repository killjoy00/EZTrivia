EZ Trivia

Sixteen categories, 2,341 questions, Quick Play, Daily Challenge, and head-to-head Friend Challenges.

Jump straight into a ten-question Quick Play mix, choose a category and difficulty, play the same Daily Challenge as everyone else, or send a Friend Challenge so someone can take on the exact same questions. Every answer includes a short explanation.

Quick Play

Ten different categories in one round, progressing from Easy to Medium to Hard.

Friend Challenges

Share a normal web link. Friends with EZ Trivia installed can jump into the exact challenge, with a short code available as a fallback.

Your progress

Daily streaks, achievements, scores, and gameplay progress can sync privately across your devices through iCloud.
