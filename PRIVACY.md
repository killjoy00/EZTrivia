# EZTrivia Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 4, 2026**

EZTrivia is a trivia game. This policy explains the information used when you play, sync progress, view ads, or use Game Center.

## Information EZTrivia uses

- **Player progress and preferences:** Scores, Daily Challenge history, Friend Challenge results, Quick Play history, seen-question state, lifetime points, and achievement progress are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, EZTrivia also uses Apple's private iCloud key-value storage to synchronize player progress across devices signed in to the same iCloud account. The app developer does not operate a separate account system or player-state server and does not receive your Apple ID credentials.
- **Game Center:** If you sign in to Apple Game Center, EZTrivia submits category lifetime-point totals, Daily Challenge weighted scores, and progress for configured Game Center achievements. Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard/achievement data under Apple's privacy policy. EZTrivia does not receive your Apple ID credentials.
- **Question reports:** If you choose **Report this question**, EZTrivia opens your email app with a draft addressed to the support email. The draft includes the question ID, prompt, selected and correct answers, explanation, difficulty, and app version so the issue can be investigated. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email.
- **Analytics and crash diagnostics:** EZTrivia ships no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and sends no gameplay events to the developer. Apple may provide the developer with aggregate App Store analytics and opt-in crash reports; that data comes from Apple, not from the app, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
- **Advertising:** Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other data needed to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. EZ Trivia requests non-personalized ads only.

## Tracking and consent

EZTrivia does not directly sell personal information. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform for applicable privacy choices and sets the non-personalized-ad flag on every banner request. Personalized advertising is not requested in this release. The app owner must keep the United States and Canada privacy messages and App Store privacy disclosures current.

## iCloud sync

iCloud player-state synchronization is provided by Apple and tied to the iCloud account configured on the device. The synchronized data is gameplay progress, not an EZTrivia account profile. If iCloud is unavailable or disabled, the app continues to work with local progress only. Requests concerning data stored or processed by iCloud should also be directed to Apple.

## Local flag artwork

National flag images are bundled with the app. Viewing a flag question does not contact a flag-image server.

## Retention and deletion

Recent category-round history can be cleared from the Scores screen. Daily Challenge history, Friend Challenge results, Quick Play history, lifetime points, and achievement facts are retained as player progress and may be synchronized through iCloud. Deleting the app removes its local copy; iCloud-synchronized key-value data may remain associated with your iCloud account under Apple's retention controls. Game Center leaderboard and achievement data is managed through Apple. Advertising retention is controlled through Google AdMob account settings and their applicable terms.

## Children

EZTrivia is not intended to collect personal information from children. The app owner must select the appropriate App Store age rating and configure AdMob child-directed treatment, age-gating, consent, and ad content settings before distribution.

## Your choices

You can play without Game Center by declining sign-in, clear recent category-round history inside the app, manage iCloud availability in iOS Settings, limit ad tracking in iOS Settings, and delete the app to remove its local data. Requests concerning data processed by Apple or Google should also be directed to those providers.

## Contact and changes

Privacy questions may be filed at <https://github.com/killjoy00/EZTrivia/issues>. Material changes will be published in this file with a revised effective date.
