The national flag PNG files under `EZTriviaApp/Assets.xcassets/Flags` were downloaded from [FlagCDN](https://flagcdn.com/) on August 23, 2026 and are stored locally so flag rounds do not make network image requests. The application integrates Apple GameKit and links the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform through Swift Package Manager. Their use is governed by the applicable Apple and Google terms and privacy policies.

## Question sources

The review catalog links to sources for the added questions. Some questions
draw on structured facts from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing),
[openfootball](https://github.com/openfootball/worldcup.json), the
[nflverse](https://github.com/nflverse/nfldata) archives, and
[FiveThirtyEight's historical NBA data](https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/data/tree/master/nba-elo).
Pronunciation puzzles use information from the
[Carnegie Mellon Pronouncing Dictionary](https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict).
